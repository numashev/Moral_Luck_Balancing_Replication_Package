# Codebook

This codebook describes the **133 variables** in `Data/AppendedData-DataFinale.rds`, the analysis dataset generated by `Data Analysis.qmd`. The raw inputs are the 26 session workbooks in `Data/Session1.xlsx`–`Data/Session26.xlsx`.

## Dataset overview

- **Unit of observation:** one participant.
- **Rows:** 1,440 participants, 180 in each of the eight treatments.
- **Columns:** 133.
- **Treatment design:** Base, Luck, and Lie Task 1 conditions crossed with Giving and Taking Task 2 frames, plus two order-reversed robustness treatments (`Giving_Lie`, `Taking_Lie`).
- **Task 1 earnings:** $0.50–$3.00.
- **Task 2 earnings:** $0.00–$3.00 kept by the participant. Higher values mean more selfish allocation; the charity receives $3.00 minus this amount.
- **Total task payoff:** `payoff` is `task1_earnings + task2_earnings`.

### Observation counts and missingness

All variables have 1,440 non-missing observations except:

- `session` — 0 (all missing; see below).
- `comment` — 161.
- `Dice_First`, `Dice_Second`, `Dice_Sum`, `Theor_Sum_freq`, `Dice_Sum_Pool_dumy`, `Theor_Sum_Pool_freq` — 720.
- `Luck_payoff` — 360.
- `SNE_order`, `SocialApp_Give_*`, `SocialApp_Take_*`, `SocialApp_T2Choice`, `SocialApp*_T2Environ` — 1,080.
- `SocialApp_DiceLie_*`, `SocialApp_DiceNoLie`, `SocialApp_DiceLie_NoGain` — 360.
- `task2_earnings_factor` — 1,379 (missing for 61 non-half-dollar Task 2 allocations).

### Coding conventions

- **Likert items** are numeric and stored as answered. Dark Triad and Big Five items use 1 = strongly disagree to 5 = strongly agree. Items marked **(R)** are already reverse-scored in the saved dataset using `6 - original response`.
- **Social appropriateness items** use 1 = very socially inappropriate, 2 = socially inappropriate, 3 = somewhat socially inappropriate, 4 = somewhat socially appropriate, 5 = socially appropriate, 6 = very socially appropriate. Participants reported the response they thought was **most common among other participants**.
- **DASS-21 items** use 1 = did not apply to me at all, 2 = applied to me to some degree or some of the time, 3 = applied to me to a considerable degree or a good part of the time, 4 = applied to me very much or most of the time. The items are stored on this 1–4 scale, not rescaled to 0–3.
- **Character fields** are either free text or coded labels; see the individual rows.

## Treatment design

| `treatment` | `treatment_num` | `treatment_label` | Task 1 | Task 2 frame |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| `Bsl_Giving` | 1 | Base–Give | Random draw | Giving |
| `Luck_Giving` | 2 | Luck-Give | Lottery | Giving |
| `Lie_Giving` | 3 | Lie–Give | Private dice | Giving |
| `Bsl_Taking` | 4 | Base–Take | Random draw | Taking |
| `Luck_Taking` | 5 | Luck-Take | Lottery | Taking |
| `Lie_Taking` | 6 | Lie–Take | Private dice | Taking |
| `Giving_Lie` | 7 | Give–Lie | Private dice, after Task 2 | Giving |
| `Taking_Lie` | 8 | Take–Lie | Private dice, after Task 2 | Taking |

In the Giving frame, the participant decides how much of a $3.00 endowment to give to the charity. In the Taking frame, the charity is endowed with $3.00 and the participant decides how much to take.
